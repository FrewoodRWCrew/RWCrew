# Walkies

In Walkies the organisation manages the walkie-talkies used during the festival.

> This module is still being built. The screens to follow up the walkie-talkies come in a next phase.

## [overview | walkies.kpi] KPI overview
Walkies' start page. This is where the key figures about the walkie-talkies will come. Until those screens exist, this page only shows a notice.

## [roles | walkies.roles] Roles
A role decides what someone may do in Walkies, per screen: **View**, **Create**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

1. Click **New role** and enter a name.
2. Click **Permissions** on the role and tick per screen what the role may do.
3. Save the permissions.

## [users | walkies.users] Users
Everyone with access to Walkies and their current role.

1. Pick a user's **role** in the drop-down. The change is saved right away.
2. Click **New user** to give someone access. If the email address doesn't exist yet, an account is created with the name and temporary password you enter.
